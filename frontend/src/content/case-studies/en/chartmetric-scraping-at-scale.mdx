## The Problem

Chartmetric's product depends on fresh, comprehensive data from every major music platform. These platforms don't offer APIs for the data we need — or their APIs are rate-limited to the point of uselessness. The only path is large-scale web scraping, but every platform actively fights it with increasingly sophisticated anti-bot defenses.

The company needed someone who could break through any wall, keep pipelines running at scale, and recover fast when platforms changed their defenses.

## Ticketmaster — The Hardest Platform

Ticketmaster is widely considered the most difficult website to scrape on the internet. Their anti-bot stack is among the most aggressive in the industry — sophisticated fingerprinting, behavioral analysis, and aggressive blocking.

I built a pipeline that reliably extracts event and ticketing data at scale. When their defenses evolve, so does the scraper. The pipeline has maintained continuous data flow since launch.

## TikTok — Recovery From Total Outage

TikTok's user ingestion pipeline went completely dark — zero data flowing for weeks. The platform had deployed new anti-scraping measures that broke the existing approach entirely.

I diagnosed the failure, engineered a new approach, and restored full data flow. The fix reduced proxy costs for TikTok alone from **$1,500/month to $50/month** while achieving higher success rates than the original pipeline.

## Genius, Shazam, Discogs, Tunefind

Each platform presents its own unique challenges — rate limiting, WAFs, fingerprinting, or all three. I built or rebuilt the scraping pipelines for all of them:

- **Genius** — Overcame aggressive rate limiting. Implemented smart budgeting per API token to maximize throughput within limits.
- **Shazam** — Built the pipeline feeding data into both PostgreSQL and ClickHouse. Diagnosed and fixed a timestamp corruption bug that had silently garbled data in Snowflake.
- **Discogs** — Built a pricing data pipeline for the marketplace, unlocking a new revenue stream.
- **Tunefind** — Consolidated two legacy pipelines (PostgreSQL and ClickHouse) into a single, unified pipeline.
